FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is PennyPal free?

Yes. PennyPal is free to download and includes unlimited transactions and categories, the overview, search, core analytics and up to 3 recurring transactions. PennyPal Pro is an optional upgrade that unlocks unlimited recurring transactions, advanced analytics and removes ads.

Where is my financial data stored?

Every transaction, category and budget you enter is stored locally on your iPhone. It is never sent to PennyPal's servers, and there are no servers that hold your financial data.

Do I need an account or to connect my bank?

No. PennyPal works without sign-up, login or bank connections. You add income and expenses yourself, and recurring ones are logged automatically on schedule.

Which devices does PennyPal support?

PennyPal is an iPhone app and requires iOS 17.6 or later. It is available on the App Store.

Can I budget weekly or in my own currency?

Yes. You can choose a weekly or monthly budget period and use any currency. The app is available in English, Arabic, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Polish, Spanish and Turkish.

How do recurring transactions work?

Add your salary, rent, insurance or subscriptions once with a schedule. PennyPal logs each one automatically when it is due, shows the next run date and a 30-day outlook, and can alert you the day before a payment.
