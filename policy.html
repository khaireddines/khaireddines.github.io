Privacy Policy for PennyPal

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PennyPal ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use PennyPal, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you enter

  • Financial data you enter manually: transactions, categories, budgets, recurring transactions and app settings.

This data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers, and we do not share it with anyone.

Information collected by third-party services

Google AdMob (ads, free plan only)

On the free plan, PennyPal shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect:

  • Device information and identifiers used for ads (not including Apple's Advertising Identifier, see below)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Interactions with ads

We do not track you. PennyPal never asks for permission to track you across other apps and websites, so Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google.

PennyPal Pro subscribers do not see ads, and no ads are requested for them.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps.

RevenueCat (subscriptions)

PennyPal uses RevenueCat to manage PennyPal Pro subscriptions. RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous app user ID created by the app
  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • Device information needed to verify purchases

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details.

Information you choose to send us

If you contact us, or send us the code shown in the app's About page (for example to receive a complimentary subscription), we receive that code, which is your anonymous RevenueCat user ID, plus anything you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you or to apply what you asked for.

On-device features

  • Motion sensor: the About page uses your device's motion sensor to detect a shake. This is processed on your device only and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Ratings: App Store rating prompts are shown by Apple. We do not receive your rating through the app.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not build profiles about you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's budgeting features (on your device)
  • To show ads on the free plan
  • To process and verify PennyPal Pro subscriptions
  • To respond to your messages and requests

3. Legal Basis (EEA/UK users)

  • Contract: to provide the app and your subscription
  • Legitimate interests: to show ads on the free plan and to keep purchases secure

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your financial data stays on your device until you delete it, by removing entries in the app or by uninstalling PennyPal. Note that iPhone and iCloud backups you make may include app data until those backups are deleted.

Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat and Apple as long as needed to manage your purchases and meet legal obligations. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your financial data. Information is shared only with:

  • Google AdMob, for ads on the free plan, as described above
  • RevenueCat, for subscription management, as described above
  • Apple, for App Store purchases

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards.

6. Security

Your financial data never leaves your device through PennyPal and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption). Services we rely on use industry-standard protections for the data they process.

7. Children's Privacy

PennyPal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data.

  • Financial data: delete entries in the app, or uninstall PennyPal to remove all of it from your device.
  • Subscription data: email us your request (with the code from the About page if you can) and we will delete your RevenueCat customer record. Purchase records held by Apple are managed by Apple.
  • Ads: you can manage ad settings in your Google account and in iOS Settings. Upgrading to PennyPal Pro removes ads entirely.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
