Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your transactions, categories, budgets and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers. Because of this, we cannot recover your data if you delete the app or lose your device, unless it is included in one of your iPhone or iCloud backups.

How do I remove ads?

Ads are shown on the free plan only. Upgrading to PennyPal Pro removes ads entirely.

I bought PennyPal Pro but it is not active

Use the restore purchases option in the app while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. If the subscription still does not appear, email us and include the code shown on the app's About page, so we can look up your purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions, select PennyPal and choose Cancel Subscription. We cannot cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I get a complimentary subscription?

Open the About page in the app and send us the code shown there, together with a short message explaining your request. The code is your anonymous user ID, which lets us apply the subscription to your account without needing any personal details.

How do I delete my data?

To delete your financial data, remove entries in the app or uninstall PennyPal. To have your subscription record deleted, email us your request, with the code from the About page if you can. See the Privacy Policy for details.
